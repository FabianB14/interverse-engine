🏃 Blob Rush

An endless runner down a rotting causeway, deeper and deeper into a swamp and out the far side — eight zones, from a misty bog to whatever The Mouth is. The road bends away out of sight and turns hard corners the camera swings round with you. Swipe to switch lanes, vault the fallen logs, duck the branches. The blob rolls; whatever hat you spend your coins on stays right where you put it.

🏃 Blob Rush 3D

The same swamp, rendered for real: the engine's new three.js renderer draws the causeway in true 3D — a sun that casts shadows, fog you can see into, corners the camera actually swings round. Same rules, same eight zones, and the same save: coins and hats carry over between this and the 2D game, because the game logic never knew which renderer it was running under.
